Idgah — inspired by Munshi Premchand (trailer)

Eid ki subah hai aur poore gaon mein full excitement hai. Bachche apni jebon mein paise count kar rahe hain. Mahmood ke paas twelve paise, Mohsin ke paas fifteen. (make it feel like a movie trailer)

Aur phir hai Hamid. Sirf four years old, nange paon, sar pe purani topi. Dadi Amina kehti hai Abba "paise kamane" gaye hain. Hamid ki jeb mein bas teen paise hain, but aankhon mein poori duniya.

*Mela. Toys, mithaiyan, jhoole. Sab bachche sweets kha rahe hain, Hamid bas dekh raha hai.*

Mohsin: "Hamid, ek bhi toy nahi liya? Kanjoos!"

Phir Hamid ek lohe ka chimta khareedta hai. Kyunki roti banate waqt Dadi ki ungliyan jal jaati hain.

Dadi Amina: "Arre pagle... ye kya le aaya?"

Teen paise. Ek chimta. Aur ek dadi ki aankhon mein aansu. Idgah. Coming this Eid.
